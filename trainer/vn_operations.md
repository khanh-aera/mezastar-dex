# Pokemon Mezastar Vietnam: operations briefing
_Compiled 2026-09-20 from the official Facebook page, the official site and regional operator pages._

## Who runs it
- **Operator: GoGo Entertainment Vietnam** (GGE holds the Pokemon licence in Vietnam). The page's contact address is on the `gge-vn.com` domain.
- Official Facebook page: **MEZASTAR Vietnam** (`facebook.com/pokemon.mezastar.vn`), Page ID 1074002785786919, created 11 March, about 10K followers, category "Game publisher".
- Direct contact: **028 3511 0456** and **mezastar@gge-vn.com**
- Official site: `world.pokemonmezastar.com/vn`. Its own news block says to **check Facebook for the latest information**, so Facebook is the primary operations channel.

## Current version
- **Stardust Version 2 only** (confirmed by Khanh 2026-09-20). Machines launched in Vietnam on 29 May 2026.
- For comparison, the regional cadence: Singapore had Stardust Version 4 from 8 January 2026 and Galaxy Version 2 from 2 July 2026, so Vietnam is running several sets behind the regional rollout.

## Venues and events
- **Pokemon MEZASTAR STADIUM at SC VivoCity, Ho Chi Minh City** is the branded play venue used for official events.
- Official tournament series: **TAG BATTLE CHALLENGE**, played in pairs (duos) and scored, not just won. Day 2 of the HCMC edition closed on **20.09.2026**; champions were Nguyen Truong Tan Sam and Nguyen Viet Hoang with **445,501 points**.
- That tournament was the first official Mezastar event in Vietnam to use **combined online and offline registration**, introduced to cut waiting times. The organisers openly asked players for feedback on the registration and operations process, which suggests the format is still being tuned.
- Timezone also operates arcades in Vietnam (for example AEON Mall Ha Dong and AEON Mall Long Bien in Hanoi), and Timezone runs Mezastar in Singapore too.

## Community channels
- Community group: `facebook.com/groups/mezastarvietnam` ("Pokemon Mezastar Vietnam")
- The page's Reels get roughly 4K to 12K views each, so the audience is active but still modest.

## Notes on access
Logged out Facebook only exposes the newest post on a page; older posts need a signed in session. Content above was read through a real browser session (Playwright) plus search indexing.
