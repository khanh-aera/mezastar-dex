# Pocket loadout card: what to bring to the arcade
_Stardust Version 2 only. Built 2026-09-20 from your binder plus the type chart. Bosses are random, so this is a blind-boss loadout._

## The default: bring these three
| Slot | Tag | PE | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lead | **Snorlax 1-2-005** | 144 | Only ONE weakness (Fighting). Safest opener, and holds your Dynamax. |
| Closer | **Kyurem 1-2-010** | 156 | Highest PE you own. 2x into Grass, Ground, Flying, Dragon. Bring it out second or third. |
| Flex | **Lucario 1-2-015** | 118 | Adds six attack types you otherwise cannot hit: Normal, Ice, Rock, Dark, Steel, Fairy. Holds your Mega Evolution. |
| | | **418 total** | Covers 10 of 18 boss types |

## If you want maximum type coverage
**Kyurem + Gardevoir + Lucario** covers **12 of 18** boss types at 398 PE. Trade off: no Snorlax tank, and Gardevoir is weak to five types.

## If you want to fire a Z-Move instead
Swap Lucario out:
- **Kyurem + Snorlax + Torterra** (412 PE, covers 10/18, Z-Move). Torterra is weak to seven types so keep it in the back.
- **Kyurem + Snorlax + Empoleon** (414 PE, covers 8/18, Z-Move). Hits Fire, Ice, Rock and Fairy that you otherwise miss.

Remember: only ONE Z-Move per session, so never carry both Torterra and Empoleon as if they were two shots. One is a wasted slot.

## Bag list (bring these as spares and swap before loading)
- **Gardevoir 1-2-007** (124): the moment you see a Fighting, Poison or Dark boss
- **Torterra 1-2-016** (112): Water, Electric, Rock, Steel, Poison, Fire bosses
- **Drednaw 1-2-025** (104): the only decent Bug answer you own
- **Meowscarada 1-2-026** (92): Psychic and Ghost bosses, your only option for both
- **Empoleon 1-2-018** (114): Fire bosses, plus your Z-Move

## Trigger budget for one session
- Dynamax: Snorlax
- Mega Evolution: Lucario
- Z-Move: Torterra OR Empoleon, once

So the cleanest plan is: Snorlax (Dynamax) + Lucario (Mega) and treat the Z-Move as a bonus only if the third tag happens to be Torterra or Empoleon.

## Lead order that works
1. **Snorlax leads** most fights: it survives almost anything and banks tempo
2. **Lucario second** when the boss is Normal, Ice, Rock, Dark, Steel or Fairy; otherwise hold it
3. **Kyurem last** as the finisher, and never against a Dragon, Fairy, Fire, Ice or Fighting boss if you can avoid it
