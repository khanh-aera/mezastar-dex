## 9. Play tactics
- Mash during the attack window; attack power builds to a cap, so late taps are wasted
- Watch the roulette multiplier; a high multiplier on a super effective move is what breaks a boss
- The catch gauge fills with damage dealt, and a Catch Chance also arrives after three turns, so keep attacking
- Ball grades Master > Ultra > Great > Normal, and the ball grade caps the star tier you can catch
- A Trainer or Memory tag in the lane opens an extra Master Ball slot
- Co-op: both players catch, both get the tag, so agree targets before the battle
- Carry only one Z-Move tag; a second one is dead weight for that session
