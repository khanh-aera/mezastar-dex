## 6. The one-per-battle budget
You may only use one of these per battle, so pick deliberately:
- **Dynamax**: Snorlax only
- **Mega Evolution**: Lucario only
- **Z-Move**: Torterra or Empoleon, and only ONE Z-Move per session

Rule of thumb: Z-Move when the boss resists your normal attacks but Torterra or Empoleon are super effective; Dynamax when you need to survive a turn and keep momentum; Mega when you need Lucario's coverage to land.

## 7. Defensive risks to respect

- **Snorlax**: only Fighting hurts it. Your safest lead.
- **Gardevoir**: Bug, Dark, Ghost, Poison, Steel all 2x. Bench it against those.
- **Lucario**: Fairy, Fighting, Fire, Flying, Ground, Psychic 2x. Fragile.
- **Empoleon**: Electric, Fighting, Fire, Grass, Ground 2x.
- **Torterra / Flapple / Appletun**: Bug, Fire, Flying, Ice, Poison, Water all hurt the Grass core.

## 9. Play tactics that actually move outcomes
- Mash during the attack window: attack power builds until the cap, so late taps are wasted
- Watch the attack roulette: a high multiplier on a super effective move is what breaks a boss
- Catch gauge fills with damage dealt; you also get a Catch Chance after three turns, so keep attacking rather than stalling
- Ball grades: Master > Ultra > Great > Normal, and the ball grade limits the star tier you can actually catch
- A Trainer or Memory tag in the lane opens an extra Master Ball slot
- Co-op: if both players catch, both get the tag, so agree targets before the battle
- Keep one Z-Move candidate benched: you cannot fire two Z-Moves in a session
